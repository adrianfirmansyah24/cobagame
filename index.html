How To Play

Game Modes

  • 🤖VS Bot — play solo against the CPU paddle
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑Multiplayer — 2 players on the same device, each enters a name

Controls

  • WSPlayer 1 — move paddle up / down
  • ↑↓Player 2 — move paddle up / down
  • 👆Touch & drag anywhere on your half of the screen
  • ESCPause / Resume the match

⚠ English Challenge

Miss the ball and the match freezes for an English question — multiple choice, 15 seconds to answer. Only the player who missed answers (if the CPU misses, it answers on its own).

  • 🛡Correct — SAVE POINT! No point is lost, play resumes.
  • ❌Wrong or time's up — opponent scores +1 point.

Every paddle hit speeds the ball up slightly. First player to reach 10 points wins the match!
